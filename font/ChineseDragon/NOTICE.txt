This file is the source typeface for the DRAGON face in Options > Font.
tools/build_fonts.py bakes it into data/ikemen1/fonts/*D*.sff, because the
engine draws its menus from bitmap fonts and cannot restyle a TrueType face
while the game runs. Keep this notice beside the font: the baked art and this
TTF are the same work.
